What We Do

Everything Your Music
Needs to Move Forward.

Winstinct is an independent artist services company helping artists and labels distribute, protect, promote and monetise their music. At Winstinct, we bring together music distribution, publishing, marketing and creative services to help independent artists build sustainable careers — on their own terms.

Your music. Everywhere.

  • Platform Pitching
  • Transparent Reporting
  • Content Protection

Publishing Administration

Make your music work harder.

  • Mechanical Royalties

  • 100% Copyright Retention
