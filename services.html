How a release runs

  1. Plan

    Eight weeks out, we agree on a release date, budget and goals with your label manager.

  2. Deliver

    Audio, artwork and metadata are checked and sent to every platform four weeks ahead.

  3. Launch

    Pitching, ads and press go live in a coordinated release week.

  4. Review

    Thirty days later we review results together and plan what comes next.
