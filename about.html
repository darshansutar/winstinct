About Winstinct

Independent
by design

We are a distribution and artist services company owned by the people who run it. No major-label parent, no outside investors setting the agenda, and no rights taken from the artists we work with.

Manifesto

Independence means artists keep their masters, labels keep their identity, and every partner can read the numbers behind their own music.

We started Winstinct because good records were getting lost between big distributors that saw them as volume and small ones that couldn't open doors. Our answer is a team big enough to reach every market and small enough to know every release by name.

What we stand for

Commitment

We plan with partners over years, not quarters. Most of our labels have been with us for more than five releases.

Craft

Metadata, artwork specs, pitch timing. We sweat the small details that decide whether a release gets found.

Transparency

Every stream, sale and fee is visible in Winstinct Studio. If we earn from your music, you can see exactly how.

How we got here

  1. 2009

    Founded in Montréal

    Three people, one shared office and twelve partner labels.

  2. 2013

    Amsterdam opens

    Our first European office, focused on electronic and club labels.

  3. 2017

    Winstinct Studio launches

    Our own catalogue and royalty dashboard replaces spreadsheets for every partner.

  4. 2021

    Seoul and Lagos

    Two new offices bring local teams to fast-growing scenes.

  5. 2026

    Over 900 partners

    Labels and artists in more than 60 countries now release with Winstinct.
