WINSTINCT

Independent
by design

Since 2009, Winstinct has helped artists and labels release music on their own terms. We are a fully independent distribution and services partner, with the reach of a major and the attention of a small team.

Learn more Read our manifesto

We work with Aurel Moss, Big Quiet, Cassia Lane, Dune Theory, Echo Parade, Fennel & Rue, Glass Orchard, Halo Wren, Ivo Marten, June Atlas, Kite Fever, Lumen Choir, Marlow Vale, Nightjar, Opal Static, Pilot Sun, Quill & Cass, Ruby Ferro, Saltwater Kings, Tamsin Rook, Umber, Velour Youth, Wilder Fields, Yellow Lanterns, Zephyr Club…

We help independent producers, artists and labels grow their audience, learn the business and build careers that last, with full ownership of their work.

A crew that

Meet the team 01 / 04

Developing an artist is a long game played by people. Our team brings together music lovers who are just as at home with data and product, guided by people who have spent decades in the industry.

Shared values and shared skills are what our reputation is built on.

Each partner gets a named label manager, not a ticket queue. The person you talk to in your first week is the person who will plan your tenth release with you.

One contact, from first single to catalogue deal.

We run listening sessions every Friday. Anyone on the team can bring a record, and plenty of our partnerships started that way.

Taste is a team sport here.

Our offices on three continents share one calendar, so a release planned in Montréal is ready for launch in Seoul and Lagos the same week.
